Bauble Cap Maker

A snap-on cap for a LithophaneMaker globe bauble. It clips over the bauble's cylinder ledge. Choose a wire pass-through for tree string lights, or a hanging spindle to wind micro LED lights on fine copper wire around inside the bauble.

String-light pass-through

Bunch the bulbs inside the bauble. Then slide the wires sideways through the slot into the centre hole, so you don't need to cut the string. The wires leave through the top of the cap.

Feed the free end of the wire down through the cap and the hollow spindle. Out of the bottom, wind it back up the groove and press the end into one of the two clip slots where the spiral finishes. Set the disc on the end of the bauble collar and snap the cap on; the cap clamps the disc in place.

The top of the spindle sits inside the bauble's cylinder, where lights would be hidden. The spiral starts below it. Make this about the cylinder height plus 3 mm. Two clip slots, one each side, hold the end of the lights where the spiral finishes.

Once the lights are wound, lay the end of the wire up the bare stem and push the clip onto the stem from the side, over the wire. It pinches the wire against the stem. Add a drop of glue if you want it permanent. If it's loose, lower the clearance.
