Height needed for 0.25 mm/px

The image height is worked out from the sphere size, keeping the same detail as the 100 mm bauble (36.25 px per mm of diameter), and the width follows from the height. Untick “Work out from sphere size” to type your own height. Angular height = 180° − asin(cylinder Ø ÷ sphere Ø), and the image is always 360° wide, so the site never crops the ends. Any height at or above the “needed” figure gives LithophaneMaker all the detail it uses; more only makes the file bigger. In 180° mode the image is still a full wrap, with your photos across the front and the back half left plain.
